Gold Master was built to give jewellers and their customers instant, accurate karat-wise gold pricing from a single daily rate — no manual math, no guesswork.

  • Auto-calculates 24K, 22K, 18K, 14K & 9K rates from one 22K input
  • Jewellery pricing with making charges, stone charges & GST
  • Saveable, shareable rate & price cards
  • Fully customizable colour themes & fonts
  • Price history log with date & time
  • Manageable promotional offers with scheduling
